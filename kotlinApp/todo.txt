# Feature Suggestions for SpinTracker

## Real-Time HUD
1. Floating HUD Overlay - Live stats window during play (not just post-session)
2. Dynamic Pop-ups - Click a player → detailed stat popup opens

## Opponent Intelligence
3. Auto Player Typing - TAG/LAG/Nit/Fish/Whale/Maniac auto-classification
4. Opponent Badges - Color-coded icons (fish, regular, whale, etc.)
5. Steal/3-bet/4-bet/Float % - Pre-defined spots with default stats
6. Fold to X% - Fold to continuation bet, double barrel, triple barrel by position
7. Call/4-bet/5-bet Range - Estimated calling ranges per opponent
8. WTSD% - Went to showdown percentage
9. W$SD% - Won money at showdown (with/without initiative)

## Advanced Statistics
10. Board Texture Heatmap - Profit by dry/mixed/wet boards
11. Confidence Intervals - Stats show "sample size too small" warnings
12. Variance Calculator - Risk of ruin, downswings depth/duration
13. Pot Commitment % - At what SPR you typically get committed
14. Aggression Frequency - Overall aggression by street
15. Donk Bet Analysis - How often you/opp donk, success rate

## Leak Buster / AI Features
16. Auto Leak Detector - Suggests "You're over-folding to turn c-bets"
17. Mistake Cost Estimator - "That play cost you €X over your sample"
18. GTO Deviation Score - How far from optimal your ranges are
19. Personalized Recommendations - "Consider 3-betting more from BTN"

## Study & Training
20. Range vs Range Equity Calculator - Integrated with hand history
21. Spot Search Engine - Natural language: "Show hands where I folded to a river shove"
22. Hand Distribution Chart - Your value vs bluff ratio visualized
23. ICM Pressure Indicator - How much ICM affects decisions at each stage
24. Bubble/Final Table Analysis - Play specifically in ITM situations

## Session & Bankroll
25. Live Session Tracker - Real-time €/hr during play
26. Bankroll Graph - With drawdown line overlaid
27. Session Notes - Color-coded notes attached to sessions
28. Stop-Loss/Stop-Win Reminders - Popup when hitting limits

## Replayer Enhancements
29. Equity Overlay - Shows equity % during hand playback
30. GTO Comparison - Your action vs what GTO would do
31. Note Stickers - Attach study notes to specific hands
